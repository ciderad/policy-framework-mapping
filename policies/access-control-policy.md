# Corporate Access Control Policy

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This [hypothetical] policy defines the requirements for granting, modifying, and revoking logical access to all corporate information systems, cloud infrastructure, and data repositories.

## 2. Policy Statements
* **Principle of Least Privilege:** Users and service accounts shall only be granted the minimum necessary permissions required to perform their authorized functions.
* **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** MFA is strictly mandatory for all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors accessing corporate environments, remote VPNs, and production cloud portals.
* **Access Revocation:** Access rights for terminated employees or contractors shall be automatically revoked within 24 hours of official notification.
